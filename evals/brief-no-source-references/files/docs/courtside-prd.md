# Courtside: product requirements document, v2

Courtside lets members of the Elm Park tennis club book courts from their phones.

- Today members book by phoning the club office, which is staffed only from 9 to 5, so evening
  courts are booked on paper at the clubhouse door.
- About 400 members; the club has six courts.
- The office spends around ten hours a week taking bookings by phone.
- Success: office time on bookings falls under two hours a week within three months of launch.
- Not in scope: coaching sessions and paying membership fees.
